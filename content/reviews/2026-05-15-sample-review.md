## Summary

This paper reviews how haptic feedback improves operator performance in remote robotic manipulation.

## Main contribution

The paper organizes haptic feedback methods into force, tactile, vibration, and thermal channels.

## Strengths

- Clear taxonomy.
- Useful comparison across robotic systems.
